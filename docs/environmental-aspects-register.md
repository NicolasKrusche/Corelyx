# Corelyx Environmental Aspects Register

**Status:** ISO 14001 preparation evidence; not a certification  
**Owner:** Corelyx responsible person / founder  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-05-27

| Aspect | Impact | Current measure | Target | Review evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cloud energy use | Energy consumption and related emissions | Prefer providers with renewable energy commitments and EU regions where feasible | Keep primary hosting on providers with published sustainability commitments | Provider sustainability pages and Green Web Foundation check |
| Developer hardware | Device manufacturing and disposal footprint | Extend device life where secure; recycle through certified e-waste channels | Document disposal for replaced devices | Disposal receipt or internal note |
| Paper use | Waste | Remote-first, no routine paper records | Maintain paper-light operation | Management review |
| Travel | Emissions | Remote-first collaboration | Avoid routine travel | Management review |
| Customer workload efficiency | Cloud resource use | Run limits, retries, and workflow controls | Avoid runaway runs and excessive model calls | Runtime limits and usage telemetry |

## Significant Aspects

Cloud energy use and customer workload efficiency are the only currently significant environmental aspects because Corelyx is a cloud-only SaaS with no office or physical production.
