# Corelyx Supplier Security Policy

**Status:** Approved internal policy, pre-certification evidence  
**Owner:** Corelyx responsible person / founder  
**Approved:** 2026-05-27  
**Review cadence:** Annual and before onboarding material subprocessors

## Purpose

Define how Corelyx selects, documents, and reviews suppliers and subprocessors that may affect customer data or service security.

## Requirements

- Maintain a public subprocessor registry with provider, purpose, data categories, region, EU-only support, transfer basis, DPA, SCC status, retention notes, activation status, and last review date.
- Maintain an internal asset and supplier register with owners and data classification.
- Review DPA, SCC, privacy, security, and region information before using a provider for customer personal data.
- Providers missing DPA/SCC/transfer-basis evidence are blocked or warned by workflow compliance checks.
- EU-only mode blocks providers that are not verified for eligible EU/EEA processing controls.
- Supplier reviews are refreshed at least annually and when a supplier materially changes region, subprocessors, or terms.

## Onboarding Checklist

| Check | Required before production personal-data use |
|---|---|
| Purpose and data categories documented | Yes |
| DPA available | Yes |
| SCC or transfer basis documented where needed | Yes |
| Region and residency limits documented | Yes |
| Retention notes documented | Yes |
| Customer activation/default status documented | Yes |
