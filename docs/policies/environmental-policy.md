# Corelyx Environmental Policy

**Status:** Approved internal policy, pre-certification evidence  
**Owner:** Corelyx responsible person / founder  
**Approved:** 2026-05-27  
**Review cadence:** Annual

## Purpose

Corelyx is a cloud-only SaaS. The environmental scope is narrow: cloud energy use, developer equipment, paper use, and travel.

## Commitments

- Prefer cloud providers and regions with published renewable energy or sustainability commitments.
- Keep the company remote-first and paper-light.
- Extend hardware life where secure and practical.
- Dispose of electronic equipment through responsible e-waste channels.
- Review environmental aspects annually and track material changes.

## Objectives

| Objective | Target |
|---|---|
| Cloud hosting | Use providers with renewable-energy commitments where technically and commercially feasible. |
| Paper | Operate with no routine paper records. |
| Travel | Prefer remote meetings; record material business travel if it becomes common. |
| Hardware | Reuse or recycle devices responsibly. |

This policy supports ISO 14001 preparation. Corelyx is not currently ISO 14001 certified.
