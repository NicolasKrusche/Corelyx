# Corelyx Incident Response Policy

**Status:** Approved internal policy, pre-certification evidence  
**Owner:** Corelyx responsible person / founder  
**Approved:** 2026-05-27  
**Review cadence:** Quarterly and after incidents

## Purpose

Define the policy requirements for detecting, responding to, communicating, and learning from security and privacy incidents.

## Policy Requirements

- Corelyx maintains an operational incident runbook in `INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md`.
- Incidents are classified SEV-1 through SEV-4 within the first 30 minutes where practicable.
- Personal data breaches are assessed against GDPR Articles 33 and 34.
- Notifiable personal data breaches are reported to the Austrian Datenschutzbehorde within 72 hours of awareness where required.
- Affected customers are notified without undue delay when an incident materially affects their data, service availability, or legal obligations. Initial customer notice should be sent within 72 hours for customer-impacting personal data incidents, even if investigation is ongoing.
- Significant incidents are assessed for NIS2 early-warning duties.
- Evidence is preserved before destructive remediation when feasible.
- SEV-1 and SEV-2 incidents require a blameless post-mortem and tracked corrective actions.

## Records

The breach register records incident ID, severity, timeline, data categories, notification decisions, root cause, remediation, and follow-up actions.
