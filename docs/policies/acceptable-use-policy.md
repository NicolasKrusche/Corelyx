# Corelyx Acceptable Use Policy

**Status:** Approved internal policy, pre-certification evidence  
**Owner:** Corelyx responsible person / founder  
**Approved:** 2026-05-27  
**Review cadence:** Annual

## Purpose

Set minimum acceptable-use rules for Corelyx systems, accounts, and customer-facing services.

## Prohibited Internal Use

- Storing secrets in source code, tickets, chat, screenshots, or logs.
- Exporting customer data to unapproved personal tools.
- Bypassing workflow compliance checks, EU-only mode, or human approval gates without documented emergency or legal-review approval.
- Using production data for training, demos, screenshots, or debugging when synthetic or redacted data is sufficient.
- Installing unreviewed browser extensions, CLI tools, or integrations with access to Corelyx production systems.

## Prohibited Customer Workflows

Corelyx may suspend or block workflows used for unlawful activity, malware, credential theft, evasion, spam, harassment, privacy violations, or prohibited AI practices. Workflows flagged as potentially prohibited under the EU AI Act must not be deployed without legal review.

## Reporting

Suspected misuse or policy violations must be reported to the responsible person or security contact immediately.
